# 🛒 Integrated Retail Analytics

An end-to-end **Retail Analytics and Machine Learning project** designed to analyze retail sales data, understand customer and product behavior, and generate actionable business insights.

The project combines **data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, customer analytics, product analysis, sales analysis, and machine learning techniques** to understand overall retail performance and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transactional data containing valuable information about customers, products, sales, and purchasing patterns.

**Integrated Retail Analytics** brings these different aspects together to provide a comprehensive view of retail business performance.

The project focuses on:

* 📊 Sales and revenue analysis
* 👥 Customer behavior analysis
* 🛍️ Product performance analysis
* 📦 Order and transaction analysis
* 📈 Business trend identification
* 🎯 Customer segmentation
* 🤖 Machine learning-based analysis

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze retail transaction data to identify important business patterns.

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customer behavior can be analyzed using purchasing characteristics such as:

* Recency
* Frequency
* Monetary value

### RFM Analysis

**RFM** stands for:

| Metric    | Meaning                           |
| --------- | --------------------------------- |
| Recency   | How recently a customer purchased |
| Frequency | How often a customer purchases    |
| Monetary  | How much a customer spends        |

RFM analysis helps identify different types of customers, such as:

* High-value customers
* Loyal customers
* Recent customers
* Occasional customers
* Customers requiring re-engagement
